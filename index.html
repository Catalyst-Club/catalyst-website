Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct fosters a welcoming, collaborative community for all members:

  • Respect for all members across all academic years
  • Constructive collaboration during meets and workshops
  • Active participation and open knowledge sharing
  • Commitment to learning, experimentation, and growth
  • Mutual support and mentorship for fellow members

About Catalyst Club

Legal Notice • Catalyst Club at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy

Terms of Use

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing catalystclub.in, submitting membership registration forms, attending club sessions, or participating in workshops at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy (HIP), you agree to abide by these Terms of Use and our Code of Conduct.

2. Organization Overview

Catalyst Club is an autonomous, student-driven scientific and computational collective at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy, ICARE Complex, Haldia. We foster innovation across pharmaceutical sciences, artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and healthcare technology.

3. Academic Integrity & Scientific Ethics

All members must uphold honest academic principles during hackathons, Python workshops, literature reviews, and seminars. Disruptive conduct will lead to revocation of membership privileges.

4. Intellectual Property & Open Source

Original computational algorithms, models, and research software developed by students remain the property of their respective student creators or are released under open-source licenses as designated by project contributors.

5. Disclaimer & Contact

Software models, data tools, and educational materials provided on this platform are for academic and learning purposes. For official inquiries, reach out to hello@catalystclub.in.

Privacy Policy • Catalyst Club at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect information voluntarily provided by students through our membership registration and contact forms, including full name, student ID, academic year, email, and contact phone number.

2. Purpose & Use of Student Data

Student details are used strictly for club administration, Saturday meet attendance records, workshop credential verification, event notifications, and certificate issuance. We never sell or share student data with commercial third parties.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Security

Attendance records and application data are securely stored using Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure, protected by database security rules accessible only by authorized Catalyst Club student coordinators.

4. Local Storage & Preferences

We use browser local storage to preserve UI theme preferences (Dark/Light Mode) and securely cache recent Saturday attendance records on your device so check-in is never lost if network connectivity fluctuates.

5. Inquiries & Rights

To inspect, correct, or request deletion of your submitted membership records, please contact our coordinator team at hello@catalystclub.in.
