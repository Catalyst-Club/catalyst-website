About Us

Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct ensures a welcoming, positive, and collaborative environment for all members:

  • Respect for all members regardless of background or academic year
  • Professional, constructive conduct during all club meets and sessions
  • Active participation and open knowledge sharing
  • Commitment to continuous learning, creative experimentation, and growth
  • Support, mentorship, and encouragement for fellow club members

About Catalyst Club

The Catalyst Club at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy is a dynamic student organization dedicated to fostering innovation, leadership, software skills, and creative collaboration. Founded with the vision of creating positive change, we provide a collaborative platform where students build impactful projects, learn modern technologies, and build lasting connections beyond the classroom.

Our Mission

Terms of Use

01 // Acceptance of Terms

By accessing catalystclub.in, submitting membership registration forms, attending club sessions, or participating in computational workshops at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy (HIP), you agree to abide by these Terms of Use and our Code of Conduct.

02 // Nature of the Organization

Catalyst Club is an autonomous, student-driven scientific and computational collective at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy, ICARE Complex, Haldia. We foster innovation at the intersection of pharmaceutical sciences, artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and healthcare technology.

03 // Academic Integrity & Scientific Ethics

All members must uphold honest academic principles during hackathons, Python workshops, scientific literature meta-analyses, and drug design seminars. Harassment, academic dishonesty, or disruptive conduct will lead to revocation of membership privileges.

04 // Intellectual Property & Open Code

05 // Disclaimer & Contact

PRIVACY POLICY // CATALYST CLUB HIP
SYS.PRIVACY // DOC_ID: CC-HIP-POL-2026 • PHARMTECH DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JANUARY 2026 • APPLIES TO CATALYSTCLUB.IN & HIP SESSIONS

01 // Information Collection

We collect information voluntarily provided by students through our membership registration and contact forms, including full name, college roll number / membership ID, B.Pharm academic year, email, and contact phone number.

02 // Purpose & Use of Student Data

Student details are used strictly for club administration, Saturday meet attendance records, workshop credential verification, event notifications, and issuance of participation certificates. We never sell or share student data with any commercial third-party entities.

03 // Cloud Database & Firestore Security

Attendance records and application data are securely stored using Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure, protected by strict database security rules accessible only by authorized Catalyst Club student coordinators.

04 // Local Storage & Offline Persistence

We use browser local storage to preserve UI theme preferences (Dark/Light Mode) and securely cache recent Saturday attendance records on your device so check-in is never lost even if network connectivity fluctuates.

05 // Inquiries & Student Rights

To inspect, correct, or request deletion of your submitted membership records, please contact our coordinator team at hello@catalystclub.in.
