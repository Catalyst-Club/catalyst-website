Eligibility Criteria

Catalyst Club welcomes motivated innovators, researchers, and creators.

  • Must be an enrolled student of Haldia Institute of Pharmacy
  • Maintain satisfactory academic standing throughout participation
  • Enthusiasm for software, pharmaceutical innovation, and tech
  • Commitment to regular attendance at Saturday meets and club workshops
BENEFITS OF MEMBERSHIP

Access to hands-on Python & computational biology bootcamps, collaborative student projects, leadership roles, and certificate opportunities.
