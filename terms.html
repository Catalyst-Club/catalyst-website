02 // Nature of the Organization

Catalyst Club is an autonomous, student-run extracurricular technical and academic club affiliated with Haldia Institute of Pharmacy, ICARE Complex, Haldia, West Bengal. The club exists to foster innovation, collaborative technical learning, scientific research, and peer leadership among students.

The website is maintained by student volunteers for non-commercial, educational, and community collaboration purposes.

03 // Membership Eligibility & Registration

Club membership is open to enrolled students of Haldia Institute of Pharmacy. Applicants must provide accurate, current, and verifiable academic details when submitting membership forms.

  • Members agree to actively participate in scheduled Saturday meets, workshops, and team projects.
  • Club leadership reserves the right to review membership status based on active participation and adherence to student conduct guidelines.
  • Membership IDs provided upon registration are personal and must not be shared or transferred.

04 // Code of Conduct & Academic Integrity

All members and visitors must uphold high standards of integrity, mutual respect, and professionalism:

  • Respect for intellectual property, academic honesty, and ethical guidelines in all technical talks, research presentations, and software projects.
  • Responsible use of campus facilities, hardware, and digital communication channels during meets and workshops.
