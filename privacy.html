Privacy Policy

How Catalyst Club collects, handles, and protects student and visitor information across our web services.

← Back to Home Privacy Policy • Catalyst Club HIP
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Catalyst Club at Haldia Institute of Pharmacy ("we", "our", or "the Club") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information you provide when using our website, applying for membership, or reaching out through our contact forms.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is utilized for club operations, and your choices regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is voluntarily submitted by users or automatically processed to operate our website:

  • Contact Form Submissions: Full name, email address, message subject, and inquiry details submitted through the "Get In Touch" section.
  • Membership Applications: Full name, academic course, year of study, section, college roll number, computer accessibility, email, phone number, and statements of interest.
  • Saturday Attendance Logging: Membership ID / college roll number logged for Saturday Meets to track student participation, sync records to cloud storage, and verify workshop credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

All collected data is used strictly for non-commercial, student club administration purposes:

  • Processing and verifying club membership applications for Haldia Institute of Pharmacy students.
  • Responding to inquiries, project collaboration requests, and support messages.
  • Broadcasting notices regarding Saturday meetings, workshops, hackathons, and guest talks to enrolled members.
  • Maintaining an internal roster of active club members and leadership roles.

4. Data Storage & Security

Contact messages and membership form data are securely processed and stored using Google Firebase Firestore cloud infrastructure with strict security rules. We implement appropriate technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Access to membership records is restricted exclusively to authorized Catalyst Club student administrators and faculty coordinators.

5. Non-Disclosure & Third Parties

We will never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information. We do not disclose student contact information to third-party advertisers or external commercial marketing agencies.

Third-party tools utilized by this site (such as Google Maps embed, Google Fonts, and Font Awesome) may collect standard technical headers (such as browser user-agent) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

6. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

This website does not utilize tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We use standard browser localStorage solely to remember your UI preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode) and sessionStorage for authenticated dashboard sessions.

7. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access to the information you have submitted, update your contact details, or request the deletion of your membership records from our active databases at any time.

To request data updates or for any privacy-related questions, please contact our team at hello@catalystclub.in.
